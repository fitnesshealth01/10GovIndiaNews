Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

GovIndiaNews respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or interact with the GovIndiaNews website and how that information may be used.

1. About this policy

GovIndiaNews is an independently operated news and information portal. We publish information about Indian government schemes, government jobs, education and examination updates.

This policy applies to information collected through the GovIndiaNews website and related website features.

2. Information you provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you contact us, submit feedback, report a correction or otherwise communicate with us.

Depending on how you contact us, this may include your name, email address, message content and other information that you choose to provide.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, government identification numbers, passwords, financial information or other confidential information through a general contact form unless specifically requested and appropriate.

3. Automatically collected information

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be automatically transmitted by your browser or device.

This may include information such as your approximate location, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and general usage information.

This information may be used to maintain website security, understand website performance, diagnose technical problems and improve the user experience.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

GovIndiaNews and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies are small files that can be stored on your device by a website. They may help remember preferences, understand website usage and support certain website functions.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.

5. Advertising

GovIndiaNews may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalise advertisements according to their own policies and applicable settings.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and manage available advertising settings through Google's own services and controls.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics or website measurement tools to understand how visitors use the website, identify technical problems and improve content and performance.

Such services may collect information about your browser, device, pages visited and interactions with the website in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

7. How information may be used

Information collected through the website may be used to:

  • respond to questions and contact requests;
  • investigate reported corrections or factual errors;
  • maintain and improve the website;
  • monitor website security and prevent abuse;
  • understand website traffic and content performance; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Information sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure or improve the website.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, court order or a legitimate government request.

9. Third-party websites

GovIndiaNews may link to official government websites and other third-party websites.

Once you leave GovIndiaNews and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites.

10. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid sending sensitive information through ordinary website forms or email.

11. Children's privacy

GovIndiaNews is a general informational website and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes that require parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. Your choices

Depending on the information involved and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information you have provided to us.

You may also control cookies and certain advertising or analytics preferences through your browser, device and relevant third-party settings.

13. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or satisfy legal obligations.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, technology, applicable requirements or our practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on GovIndiaNews, please contact us through our Contact page .

16. Related policies

You may also wish to review our Terms of Service and About Us page for information about our editorial practices and website.

Disclaimer: GovIndiaNews is a privately run news and information portal. Content is provided for general informational purposes and is compiled from publicly available government notifications, press releases and official websites. We are not a government entity and cannot process applications, payments, or grievances on behalf of any government body. Always verify scheme and recruitment details on the concerned department's official website before applying or making any payment.
