About GovIndiaNews

Last updated: August 19, 2026

GovIndiaNews is an independently run news and information portal focused on Indian government schemes, sarkari (government) job notifications, and education and exam updates.

We are not a government website, and we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the Government of India, any state government, or any government department.

What we do

Our team reads through official notifications, press releases and scheme guidelines, and rewrites them in plain, accessible language — covering who is eligible, how to apply, what documents are needed, and key deadlines.

Every article links back to the original government source so readers can verify details for themselves.

What we don't do

  • We do not process applications, collect application fees, or issue admit cards, offer letters, or certificates.
  • We do not charge for information — our content is free to read and supported by advertising.
  • We do not guarantee outcomes such as job selection, scheme approval, or exam results.
